# Financial Statement Analysis of Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)

**Course:** MBA 601 – Accounting & Finance

**Institution:** Department of Management Sciences (DoMS), IIT Kanpur

**Instructor:** Dr. Harshal Ranjan Mulay

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the financial performance of **Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)** using five years of standalone financial statements (FY2019–20 to FY2023–24). The study evaluates the company's profitability, liquidity, solvency, efficiency, and overall financial position through ratio analysis and the DuPont framework.

The objective was to understand how TCS's financial performance evolved over time, particularly through the COVID-19 period and the subsequent recovery.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze TCS's financial statements over a five-year period.
- Evaluate profitability, liquidity, solvency, and operational efficiency.
- Assess changes in financial performance before and after COVID-19.
- Interpret financial trends using ratio analysis and DuPont decomposition.

---

## Scope of Analysis

### Profitability
- Gross Profit Margin
- EBITDA Margin
- EBIT Margin
- EBT Margin
- Net Profit Margin
- Return on Equity (ROE)
- Return on Assets (ROA)

### Liquidity
- Current Ratio
- Quick Ratio
- Cash Ratio

### Solvency
- Debt-to-Equity Ratio
- Total Debt Ratio
- Interest Coverage Ratio
- Debt Service Coverage Ratio
- Equity Multiplier

### Efficiency
- Receivables Turnover
- Payables Turnover
- Working Capital Cycle
- Cash Conversion Cycle
- Fixed Asset Turnover
- Total Asset Turnover

### DuPont Analysis
- ROE = Profit Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier

---

## Methodology

1. Collected standalone financial statements of TCS for FY2019–20 to FY2023–24.
2. Calculated key financial ratios using Microsoft Excel.
3. Performed trend analysis across the five-year period.
4. Applied DuPont Analysis to examine the drivers of Return on Equity.
5. Interpreted results in the context of business events such as the COVID-19 pandemic, employee cost growth, and changes in capital structure.

---

## Key Findings

- TCS experienced temporary pressure on growth during the COVID-19 period but recovered strongly in subsequent years.
- Profitability remained consistently strong, supported by the company's asset-light business model.
- Higher employee costs affected operating margins, although overall profitability remained healthy.
- ROE improved alongside ROA, indicating that improved returns were driven primarily by operational performance rather than excessive leverage.
- Liquidity remained comfortable despite moderate declines in current ratios.
- Asset utilization and operational efficiency improved steadily over the analysis period.

## Conclusion

The analysis indicates that TCS maintained strong financial stability throughout the study period. Despite temporary disruptions during COVID-19, the company demonstrated resilient profitability, effective financial management, and sustained operational efficiency, reinforcing its position as one of India's leading IT services companies.
